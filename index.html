FAQ

Questions parents often ask.

What ages are Pine Tree art classes for?

Our classes are for children aged 4 to 12.

Which classes do you offer?

We offer Clay Art Level 1 (shaping, colour mixing and fun clay miniatures), Clay Art Level 2 (bigger scenes and finer detail, after completing Level 1) and a Drawing Course (lines, shapes and colour, from first sketches to finished artwork).

Where and when are classes held?

Classes run on weekends at two locations in Johor Bahru: Taman Molek (at Brilliant Stars Preschool) and Mutiara Rini (at Lotus's Mutiara Rini, inside Seven Castle playground).

Are art materials included?

Yes. All art materials are included, and every finished piece goes home with your child.

Can my child try a class first?

Yes. We offer trial classes. Message us on WhatsApp at +60 11-3660 2096 to book one.

How much do classes cost?

For current class fees and times, message us on WhatsApp at +60 11-3660 2096 and our team will get back to you.

Do children receive a certificate?

Yes. Our programmes are structured in levels, and children receive a certificate of completion that recognises their progress.

Do you run workshops for schools, birthdays and companies?

Yes. We run themed craft workshops, parent-child activities, school holiday workshops, birthday and private events, corporate family days, and sessions for schools and kindergartens across Johor.

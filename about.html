About Pine Tree

Art is a chance to explore, express and discover.

At Pine Tree Learning Hub, we believe art is more than making beautiful things. It is a chance for children to explore ideas, express themselves and find out what they are capable of.

Our programmes give children hands-on time to learn artistic techniques, experiment with materials and develop their own ideas, in a supportive space where they learn at their own pace and feel proud of what they make.

Our mission

To nurture children's creativity and artistic abilities through meaningful, engaging and hands-on learning experiences.

Our vision

To inspire a generation of confident, imaginative young learners who explore their potential through art.

A creative journey

From start to finish, and on to the next level.

We value the creative process and the satisfaction of finishing a project. Each finished artwork is a reminder of a child's effort, imagination and progress.
